# Replace the meadow WebP with the new PNG

## Summary

The owner supplied a new `static/meadow-day.png` (1672 x 941, deep blue sky) to replace `meadow-day.webp`. Every art reference now points to the PNG, and two contrast fixes follow from the darker sky.

## Changes

- `static/meadow-day.webp`: removed; `static/meadow-day.png`: added.
- `src/app.css`: background URL to PNG; Day sidebar glass 84% to 88%; window buttons (`.tb-controls`) on an `over-art` plate.
- `src/routes/onboarding/+page.svelte`, `src/routes/m/+layout.svelte`, `src/routes/m/pair/+page.svelte`: art URL to PNG.
- `DESIGN.md`: asset row, glass token, Title bar plate, contrast table.

## Decisions

- Sidebar glass raised because `ink-2` fell to 4.42:1 over the new sky at 84%.
- Window button icons fell to 1.9:1 over the Day sky, so they sit on a plate (11.04:1 now).

## Verification

- Pixel simulation, 1100 to 2560 wide: sidebar `ink-2` 4.80 Day / 6.31 Dusk; text plates 5.82 / 7.03; title bar icons 11.04 / 11.79.
- `bun run check`: 0 errors. `bun run build`: ok, only `meadow-day.png` in `build/`. `bun run test`: 105 passed.

## Limitations

- PNG is 2.7 MB against 100 KB for the WebP; the phone page loads it over the LAN.
- Focus rings (`forest`) over the bare Day painting are low contrast on dark grass; not fixed here.

## Follow-up

- Consider a high-quality WebP of the new image; fix focus rings over the painting.
