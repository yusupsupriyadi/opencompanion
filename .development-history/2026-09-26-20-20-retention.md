# Retention for finished sessions (FR-62) and a complete Delete history

## Summary

Settings › History gets "Keep finished sessions for" (Forever by default, 90, 30, 7, 1 day). Code review also found that "Delete finished sessions" left every terminal log (which can hold secrets) and hook file on disk, kept Board and Chat links to the deleted sessions, and left them in the sidebar and Overview until restart.

## Changes

- `src-tauri/src/db.rs`: `Settings.keep_days`; `finished_sessions(before)` replaces `delete_history`; `delete_session` also clears the session from Chat cards. Tests.
- `src-tauri/src/session.rs`: `prune(keep_days)`, `delete_finished()` (per session, with its log, hook and hook settings files), and a sweep of files whose session is gone. Test with old, recent and running sessions plus leftover files.
- `src-tauri/src/lib.rs`: retention at start and every hour, and at once when Settings changes it; every deletion emits `session-deleted` and a phone resync.
- `src/routes/settings/+page.svelte`: the select and its line; Delete refreshes the lists. `api.ts`, test.
- PRD FR-62, DESIGN D7 History card, README.
