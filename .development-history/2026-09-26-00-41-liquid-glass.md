# Liquid glass on cards, panels, plates, dialogs, buttons and inputs

## Summary

The owner asked for cards and other components to become transparent liquid glass, choosing the AA-safe level (Day 82%) and every scope offered. This overrides the old one-glass-panel rule in DESIGN.md and the antislop R-10 dose cap, recorded as an owner decision.

## Changes

- `src/app.css`: glass tokens per theme (`glass`, `glass-2`, `glass-bg`, `glass-pop`, `glass-toast`, `glass-rim`, `glass-blur`, `solid`); one material rule for every container over the painting that blurs and saturates the backdrop and redefines `surface`, `surface-2` and `bg` inside, so nested buttons, inputs and rows turn translucent; pop-ups use `glass-pop`; error text on a small `solid` plate; dialog and toast glass; unused `over-art` removed.
- `src/lib/PlannerModel.svelte`: borderless picker select opts out of the rim.
- `src/routes/board/+page.svelte`: Delete card button text on `solid`.
- `src/routes/onboarding/+page.svelte`: panel uses the glass material.
- `DESIGN.md`: glass tokens, liquid glass rule and override, contrast table.

## Decisions

- Tints are the thinnest that keep `ink-2` at 4.5:1 over the blurred painting; pop-ups are not blurred (their glass parent is the backdrop root), so they take the tint that passes unblurred.
- Primary buttons, status chips and terminals stay solid.

## Verification

- Pixel simulation, blur 24 + saturate 1.4, 1100 to 2560 wide: cards 4.67 Day / 4.66 Dusk, sidebar 4.69 / 4.81, `glass-2` 4.63 / 4.63, `glass-bg` 4.67 / 4.76, pop-ups unblurred 4.94 / 5.45.
- `bun run check`: 0 errors. `bun run build`: ok. `bun run test`: 105 passed.

## Limitations

- Not viewed in the running app. Many backdrop filters may cost GPU time while scrolling long lists.
- The `.rail.pop` rim edit sits inside another agent's uncommitted rewrite of `src/routes/chat/+page.svelte` and was left for them.
- Focus rings over the bare painting (outside any glass) are still low contrast on dark grass.
