# Tray mode: closing the window keeps sessions running (FR-18)

## Summary

Closing the window used to quit the app and kill every session. It now hides the window to a tray icon while Settings keeps "Keep running in the tray" on (the default), so sessions and the phone connection carry on. The tray icon opens the window; its menu opens it or quits, and quitting is what stops the sessions.

## Changes

- `src-tauri/Cargo.toml`: tauri `tray-icon` feature (API checked against the Tauri 2 docs through Context7).
- `src-tauri/src/lib.rs`: `tray` (icon, tooltip, Open and Quit menu, left click opens), `on_close` (prevent close and hide while `close_to_tray`; a one-time notification says where the app went), `show_main` shared by the tray, a second launch and notifications.
- `src-tauri/src/db.rs`: `close_to_tray` (default on), `tray_hint_shown`. Test.
- `src/routes/settings/+page.svelte`: "Closing the window" card. `api.ts` types. Test.
